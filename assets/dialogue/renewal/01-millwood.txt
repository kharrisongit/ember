@ Elder Maddock|Millwood|Elder and Corin's mentor

= When I gave up travelling|I stopped travelling after I injured my knee. For months I called it a temporary delay. Eventually I planted beans.|Was staying here hard?|At first. = An unreliable map|I once followed a map to a bridge that had washed away fifteen years earlier. Then I walked two days to the next crossing, with a very quiet mouth.|Should I trust our map?|Use it, but listen when the road disagrees. A drawing cannot tell you what happened yesterday.
= The seven riders|There were seven riders before Wingfall. Halvard was one of them. = Why help me?|I gave you the sword because the woods were dangerous. I didn't expect it to put you on a road to Cinderhold.|Do you wish you'd kept it?|I wish you didn't need it. = A memory of Mum|Your mother hated singing in front of people, but she sang while she mended clothes. If I entered, she would pretend she had been humming.|Was she any good?|Lovely voice. = Your own adventures|I went to a dance in Thornwell once without telling my mother. I thought I'd be home before she noticed.|Did you make it?|I found her there.
